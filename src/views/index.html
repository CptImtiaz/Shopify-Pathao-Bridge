    Common questions

    Why wasn't an order sent to Pathao automatically?

    Auto-send skips orders created before it was turned on, cancelled orders, orders with the skip tag, and high-risk orders when “Hold high-risk orders” is on. These show under Needs action with the reason — press Send or Review.

    Pathao says the phone number is invalid

    Pathao accepts Bangladesh mobile numbers like 01XXXXXXXXX. Fix the phone on the Shopify order, then press Retry.

    Delivery status isn't changing

    Set up Live delivery updates in Couriers → Pathao. Once Pathao sends its first update, orders show a green Live sign.

    Shopify orders aren't marked fulfilled

    Turn on Update orders in Shopify and allow the fulfillment permission when Shopify asks. Orders booked before that are updated automatically afterwards.

    How does the fraud check work?

    It combines Shopify's risk analysis with the customer's delivery history in your store, phone and address checks, repeat orders from the same phone, and large cash-on-delivery amounts.

    How am I billed?

    Plans are charged on your regular Shopify bill. You can switch or cancel on the Billing page; paid plans start with a free trial.
