Privacy Policy – ShipBridge for Pathao

Last updated: [DATE]

ShipBridge for Pathao ("the App") is provided by [YOUR COMPANY NAME] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App accesses when a Shopify merchant installs it, and how that data is used.

Data we access

  • Order data from your Shopify store: order number, line items, weights, totals, outstanding balance, order note and tags, and the shipping recipient's name, phone number and address.
  • Your Pathao Merchant API credentials, which you enter in the App.

How we use it

Order data is used only to create delivery orders in your own Pathao merchant account, on your instruction (manually or via the auto-send setting you enable). It is sent directly to Pathao's Merchant API.

What we store

  • Shopify order IDs and order numbers, Pathao consignment IDs, delivery status, cash-to-collect amount and any error message.
  • Your settings and Pathao credentials, encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
  • Shopify access tokens, encrypted.

We do not store customer names, phone numbers, emails or addresses. They are read from Shopify at the moment an order is sent and passed to Pathao.

Sharing

Data is shared only with Pathao Ltd. (to fulfil deliveries) and our hosting provider. We never sell data.

Retention and deletion

When you uninstall the App, your Pathao credentials and Shopify tokens are deleted immediately, and all remaining store data is deleted when Shopify sends the shop/redact request (48 hours after uninstall). We honour Shopify's customer data and redaction requests.
